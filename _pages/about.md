<div class="lang-en" markdown="1">
I am Huang Yuchang (黄宇场), an M.Eng. student in Electronic Information at **Tianjin University** (enrolled in Fall 2025). Currently, I work as a Backend Development Intern at **ByteDance (Volcano Engine)**, building automated evaluation self-repair pipelines and multi-source knowledge bases for internal Team Assistant agents. I am also a Research Intern at the **MiaoShou Research Group, Renmin University of China**, working on empirical paper generation agents and base-model post-training.

My research and engineering interests lie in **AI for Software Engineering (AI4SE)** and **AI for Science (AI4Science)**, focusing on: (1) combining structured engineering workflows with multi-agent collaboration for software delivery and production troubleshooting; (2) execution-grounded long-form scholarly document generation; and (3) fact-consistent base-model post-training for quantitative academic text. See my <a href="{{ '/assets/pdf/CV_en.pdf' | relative_url }}">CV</a>, <a href="{{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}">publications</a>, and <a href="{{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}">experience</a> for details.

Outside of daily research and engineering, I enjoy AI hackathons, open-source development, and mathematics competitions—receiving Second Prize at the 2026 Global AI Innovation, Governance, and Safety Competition, the Open Source Contribution Award at Alibaba's "AI for Good" Challenge, the AI Rising Star Certificate (Final Interview Fast-Track) at the Feishu AI Campus Challenge, First Prize (Top 5%) in the Chinese Mathematics Competition, and National Champion in the GE Lean & Green Challenge. Feel free to reach out via email or [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/huangyuchang/).
