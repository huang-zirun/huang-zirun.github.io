<div class="lang-en" markdown="1">
I am Huang Yuchang (黄宇场), an M.Eng. student in Electronic Information at **Tianjin University** (enrolled in Sep 2025). Currently, I am a Backend Development Intern at **ByteDance (Volcano Engine)**, after completing a prior internship at **ByteDance (Feishu / Lark)** (Jun 2026 – Oct 2026), focusing on internal AI Team Assistants, automated evaluation & self-iteration pipelines, knowledge base distillation, and team-scale requirement delivery harnesses. Since Dec 2025, I have also been a Research Intern at the **MiaoShou Research Group, Renmin University of China**, working on execution-grounded empirical paper generation agents and fact-consistent base-model post-training.

My research and engineering interests focus on **AI for Software Engineering (AI4SE)** and **AI for Science (AI4Science)**. Specifically, I explore combining deterministic engineering workflows with bounded multi-agent autonomy for automated software delivery, alongside execution-grounded scholarly generation and quantitative modeling. See my <a href="{{ '/assets/pdf/CV_en.pdf' | relative_url }}">CV</a>, <a href="{{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}">publications</a>, and <a href="{{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}">experience</a> for more details.

Beyond daily research and engineering, I actively participate in AI hackathons, open-source projects, and mathematical competitions—having received Second Prize at the 2026 Global AI Innovation, Governance, and Safety Competition, the Open Source Contribution Award at Alibaba's "AI for Good" Innovation Challenge, the AI Rising Star Certificate at the Feishu AI Campus Challenge, First Prize (Top 5%) in the Chinese Mathematics Competition, and National Champion in the GE Lean & Green Challenge. You can also find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/huangyuchang/).
