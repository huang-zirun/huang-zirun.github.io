<div class="lang-en" markdown="1">
I am Huang Yuchang (黄宇场), an M.Eng. student in Electronic Information (Control Engineering) at **Tianjin University** (enrolled in Fall 2025). Currently, I work as a Backend Development Intern at **ByteDance (Volcano Engine)**, building automated evaluation self-repair pipelines and multi-source knowledge bases for internal Team Assistant agents. I am also a Research Intern at the **MiaoShou Research Group, Renmin University of China**, focusing on LLM post-training and multi-agent systems.

My work spans **AI for Software Engineering (AI4SE)** and **AI for Science (AI4Science)**. On the engineering side, I build backend infrastructure for production AI agents—covering multi-agent delivery harnesses, offline evaluation, knowledge distillation, and sandbox self-repair loops. On the research side, I explore execution-grounded generation, base-model post-training (LoRA / SFT), and fact-consistency verification. For more details, see my <a href="{{ '/assets/pdf/CV_en.pdf?v=20261122-3' | relative_url }}">CV</a>, <a href="{{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}">publications</a>, and <a href="{{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}">experience</a>.

Outside of daily work, I enjoy AI hackathons, open-source projects, and mathematics competitions—receiving Second Prize at the 2026 Global AI Innovation, Governance, and Safety Competition, the Open Source Contribution Award at Alibaba's "AI for Good" Challenge, the AI Rising Star Certificate (Final Interview Fast-Track) at the Feishu AI Campus Challenge, First Prize (Top 5%) in the Chinese Mathematics Competition, and National Champion in the GE Lean & Green Challenge. Feel free to reach out via email or [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/huangyuchang/).
