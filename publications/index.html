  1. SCIS
    EmpiricalPaperForge: A Statistical Method-Aware System for Empirical Papers EmpiricalPaperForge：面向实证论文的统计方法感知生成系统
    Huang Yuchang (Second Author, Advisor as First Author) 黄宇场（第二作者，导师第一作者）
    Submitted to SCIENCE CHINA Information Sciences (SCIS, CCF-A / SCI Q1), Under Review, 2026 投稿至 SCIENCE CHINA Information Sciences（《中国科学：信息科学》SCIS，CCF-A / SCI 一区），在审，2026
    AbstractElevates executable statistical computation to a first-class evidence source for empirical paper generation—orchestrating 16+ econometric method plugins across 6 real-world economics and social science datasets into a typed Asset Pool, paired with dynamic outline planning, TF-IDF reference binding, placeholder asset injection, and numerical fact-checking. 摘要将可执行统计计算作为实证论文生成的一等证据源，封装 16+ 种计量经济学方法插件并对接 6 个真实经管与社科数据集生成结构化资产池（Asset Pool），结合动态大纲规划、TF-IDF 文献检索绑定、占位符图表注入与数值回溯核对，解决大模型写实证论文时依赖参数记忆编造统计结果的问题。
  2. ACL 2027
    Fact-Consistent Base Model Post-Training for Quantitative Academic Text Generation 面向跨学科定量学术文本的基座模型后训练与事实一致性核查研究
    Huang Yuchang, et al. 黄宇场 等
    Working Paper / Manuscript in Preparation (Targeting ACL 2027), 2026 在研工作（目标投稿 ACL 2027），2026
    AbstractInvestigates how to restore human scholarly language distributions via high-rank LoRA post-training on unaligned Base Models (Qwen3-4B-Base) across 3,521 zero-overlap syntactic restructuring pairs from 4 quantitative disciplines while guaranteeing 100% statistical and factual fidelity; introduces an academic DeBERTa-v3-Large verifier trained on 12,580 contrastive samples (validation AUROC 0.998, 100% multi-number cross-binding detection) and multi-domain discriminator probes trained on 9,330 samples. 摘要针对指令微调模型在定量学术写作中的句式模板化及改写过程中的统计数值失真问题，构建覆盖经管、计算机、临床生物医学与统计工程 4 学科的 3,521 对零重叠句式重组语料对 Qwen3-4B-Base 进行高秩 LoRA 后训练；构造 12,580 条对比样本微调学术版 DeBERTa-v3-Large 事实一致性核查模型（验证集 AUROC 达 0.998，同句多数值交叉错配 100% 识别）并联合 9,330 条样本训练多领域判别探针，在 465 段跨学科测试集上实现 100% 统计数据零出错。
